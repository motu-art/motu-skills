# 动物 / 宠物摄影 — animal

**何时用**：宠物摄影（猫/狗/异宠）、野生动物感画面、萌宠商业图、宠物品牌
内容。命门是**眼睛清晰 + 解剖正确**。

## 填充清单

| 字段 | 设计要点 |
|---|---|
| 物种品种 | 什么动物、什么品种（英短/柯基/橘猫） |
| 特征 | 毛色花纹、年龄感、体型 |
| 动作 | 在做什么（扑/卧/回眸/奔跑）——决定神态 |
| 神态 | 耳朵/胡须/瞳孔状态；**眼睛必须清晰锐利** |
| 环境 | 室内窗光/草地/影棚纯色/自然栖息地 |
| 光线 | 窗光/逆光轮廓毛边/柔光棚拍 |
| 相机 | 70–200mm、浅景深、平视机位（宠物视角） |
| 毛发质感 | 逆光下的绒毛边、可见毛流 |
| 禁止 | 错误解剖、多余/畸形肢体、畸形五官、塑料毛发感 |

## Z-Image 骨架

```text
title: [名字/神态], A pet photograph of a [品种] [动物] with [毛色与体型特征].,
[4-6 氛围词: calm, endearing, plush, serene …],
[动作: loaf position with front paws tucked, one ear rotated toward camera],
[神态: whiskers fanned forward, level direct gaze, both eyes tack-sharp],
[环境: bright minimalist living room, linen sofa blurred far behind],
[配色: blue-gray, pale oak, cream],
[毛发质感: dense plush coat with visible fur direction, fine halo along ear edges],
full_frame, 135mm, f/2.2, ISO 400, shutter speed: 1/500,
medium close-up, [画幅] at the animal's eye level, subject filling the frame,
shallow, background melting into warm neutrals,
Soft Window Light, large window, sheer curtain diffusion, position: camera left, backlit halo along the rounded ear edges, intensity: soft,
[辅光: gentle bounce fill, ratio: 2:1, lifting the shadow under the chin],
soft, warm, clean,
pet photography, realistic fur and anatomy,
[调色: natural tones, contrast: soft, saturation: moderate, warm neutrals],
[调子词: calm, endearing],
no wrong anatomy, no extra limbs, no deformed face, no plastic-looking fur,
no text, no watermark
```

## 填写要点

- `both eyes tack-sharp` 必写——动物摄影的第一评价标准就是眼睛；失焦眼=废片。
- 平视机位（`at the animal's eye level`）是宠物片显专业的最大单一变量，默认
  俯拍会显得像手机随手拍。
- 逆光 + 绒毛边（`backlit halo along the ear edges`）出氛围；正面柔光出清洁
  商业感。
- 动物解剖负面固定：`no wrong anatomy, no extra limbs, no deformed face` +
  品种混写要小心（写英短就别加折耳特征）。
