# 时尚编辑大片 — fashion

**何时用**：服装品牌 lookbook、杂志编辑大片、造型展示、时尚街拍。**服装与造型
是主角，人是衣架**——这是与 [portrait.md](portrait.md)（人本身是主角）的分界。
全身、姿势感、场景叙事。

## 填充清单

| 字段 | 设计要点 |
|---|---|
| 服装 | 逐件：品类 + 面料 + 廓形 + 颜色（oversized 廓形是关键信息） |
| 造型 | 妆容 + 发型（湿发/高马尾/羊毛卷）+ 配饰 |
| 模特气质 | 年龄段、身形、族裔、台风（清冷/张扬/慵懒） |
| 姿势 | editorial pose：靠墙/行进中转身/坐姿伸展——写关节角度 |
| 表情视线 | 直视镜头/侧脸望远/闭眼仰头 |
| 场景 | 秀场后台/街头/极简影棚/废弃工厂/海边 |
| 相机 | 80mm 中画幅感、全身、平视或低机位拉比例 |
| 灯光 | 硬闪（时尚感）/大柔光（高级感）/自然光（街拍） |
| 色彩 | 服装色 × 环境色的关系（撞色/同色系） |
| 禁止 | 畸形手/多指、假人感塑料皮肤、文字、水印 |

## Z-Image 骨架

```text
title: [大片名], A fashion editorial photograph of a [气质] model in [服装一句话].,
[4-6 氛围词: assertive, quiet-luxury, graphic, modern …],
[服装: oversized cognac leather trench with dropped shoulders, fine-knit turtleneck, wide-leg wool trousers, square-toe boots],
[造型: wet-look slicked-back bun, bronze glossy lip, small gold hoops],
human, [gender], age range: [N-N], [ethnicity], [build: statuesque, long lines],
[pose: striding mid-step toward camera, one hand in pocket, other pulling the collar, chin slightly down],
[expression: level unbothered gaze direct to camera],
[环境: bare concrete pillars and yellow parking-line floor under skylight],
[配色: cognac and chocolate against concrete gray, yellow line accent],
full_frame, 80mm, medium-format look, f/4.0, ISO 100, shutter speed: 1/200,
full-body, vertical, slightly low angle lengthening the figure, outfit sharp,
Hard Flash Key Light, on-camera flash, position: camera left, casting a crisp full-length shadow on the wall, intensity: hard,
crisp, graphic, editorial,
fashion editorial photography, magazine cover quality,
[调色: warm leather tones against cool gray, contrast: medium-high, saturation: moderate],
[调子词: quiet-luxury, assertive],
no deformed hands, no extra fingers, no plastic skin, no text, no watermark
```

## 填写要点

- 服装组「面料 + 廓形 + 颜色」三件缺一不可：`oversized raw-denim jacket with
  dropped shoulders` 里的廓形词决定整张图的时尚度。
- 姿势组写关节不写氛围：`leaning against the wall with one knee bent and chin
  lifted`，比 `很酷的姿势` 可执行一万倍。
- 硬闪 + 影子 = 时尚感（`hard flash casting a crisp shadow on the colored wall`）；
  高级感 = 大柔光 + 低饱和；街拍 = 自然光 + 环境。
- 低机位（`slightly low angle lengthening the figure`）是编辑大片常用比例技巧。
