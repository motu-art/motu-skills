# Logo / 品牌视觉 — logo

**何时用**：品牌 Logo、icon、标志概念稿。注意：生图给的是**概念方向**，不是可用
的矢量文件 —— 定稿后仍需设计师在 AI 里重绘。

## 填充清单

| 字段 | 必答 |
|---|---|
| 品牌名称 | 是什么（也会作为 wordmark 渲染进图里，逐字引号） |
| 行业 | AI/科技/汽车/教育/金融/医疗 |
| 品牌属性 | 专业/可信/创新/未来/简单/高端 |
| 核心概念 | A + B 结合（如 Magic + Camera） |
| 图形 | 简洁几何、强识别度、小尺寸可辨 |
| 结构 | 图形 Logo + Wordmark；图形左、名称右 |
| 颜色 | 主色 + 辅色（≤2 色） |
| 应用 | 网站 / App Icon / favicon / 名片 / 社媒 —— 必须小尺寸可用 |
| 禁止 | 复杂插画、3D 效果、过度渐变、阴影、照片质感、多余装饰 |

## Z-Image 骨架

```text
title: [品牌名] Logo, A minimal vector logo design for [品牌名], a [行业] brand.,
minimal, geometric, modern, professional, scalable,
clean flat white background,
[主色], [辅色],
[核心概念组: [A] combined with [B] as a single geometric mark],
[图形组: simple geometric shapes, strong distinct silhouette, instantly recognizable, legible at small favicon size],
[结构组: logo mark on the left, wordmark text "[品牌名逐字]" on the right in clean geometric sans-serif],
[应用组: suitable for website, app icon, favicon, business card and social media],
flat vector logo design, unified stroke weight, balanced optical spacing,
Even Flat Lighting, no shadows or gradients, intensity: flat and uniform,
none, flat graphic only,
clean, flat, precise,
minimal vector logo, professional brand identity,
[配色: limited two-color palette, contrast: high, saturation: restrained],
precise and balanced,
[叙事句],
[情绪词],
exact spelling as quoted in wordmark, no 3d effects, no gradients,
no shadows, no photorealistic textures, no complex illustration,
no extra ornaments, no watermark
```

## 填写要点

- 用 `square` preset —— logo 展示永远是方的。
- 一稿一个概念方向；要多个方向就逐张设计不同的 A+B 概念，而不是 batch。
- wordmark 文字逐字引号 + `exact spelling as quoted`；品牌名拼写错是最常见事故。
- 平面感是硬约束：`flat, no 3d effects, no gradients, no shadows` 三连。
