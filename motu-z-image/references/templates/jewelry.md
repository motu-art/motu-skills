# 珠宝 / 美妆小件 — jewelry

**何时用**：戒指、项链、耳饰、手表、香水瓶、口红、精华瓶等**小微距 + 高级
光泽**特写。与 [product.md](product.md) 的分界：反射控制与材质光泽是命门，
微距景深、束光、吸光都是这个模板的专属工具。

## 填充清单

| 字段 | 设计要点 |
|---|---|
| 品类 | 戒指/项链/香水瓶/口红/精华… |
| 材质 | K金/铂金/银、宝石切工（oval cut/梨形）、磨砂玻璃、漆面 |
| 状态 | 悬浮/卧放/佩戴局部（手模耳模） |
| 背景 | 黑丝绒/白亚克力/水/石材/镜面 |
| 反射控制 | 长条柔光高光沿金属、黑卡吸光勾暗缘 |
| 微距 | 100mm macro、极浅景深、焦点在爪镶/刻字/切面 |
| 细节 | 水珠、气泡、飘丝、指纹禁绝 |
| 光线 | 束光/点光 + 黑背景 = 珠宝感；窗光 + 白背景 = 清新美妆 |
| 禁止 | 错误结构、塑料金属感、灰尘指纹、杂乱反射、文字水印 |

## Z-Image 骨架

```text
title: [珠宝名], A macro product photograph of a [品类] in [材质细节].,
[4-6 氛围词: luxurious, precise, sculptural, intimate …],
[结构: oval-cut diamond, six-claw setting, brushed platinum band],
[状态: upright on wet black slate / suspended / worn on a hand model],
[背景台面: deep black velvet, faint reflection on polished slate],
[细节: two tiny water droplets beside the piece, zero dust, zero fingerprints],
[配色: cool platinum white, deep black],
[材质质感: satin-brushed metal, faceted stone with points of fire],
full_frame, 100mm macro, f/8.0 focus-stacked, ISO 100, shutter speed: 1/200,
extreme close-up, [画幅], subject centered,
micro, background falling to pure black,
Strip Softbox Key Light, long strip softbox, position: above and left, drawing an unbroken highlight around the band, intensity: controlled,
Black Flag, [作用: carving the dark lower edge for contrast], position: camera right bottom,
Spark Beam Accent, focused beam, position: camera top right, sparking the stone's facets,
precise, crisp, luxurious,
macro jewelry photography, jewelry catalog lighting,
[调色: cool metallics on deep black, contrast: high, saturation: moderate],
[调子词: luxurious, precise],
no incorrect structure, no plasticky metal, no dust, no fingerprints,
no distracting reflections, no text, no watermark
```

## 填写要点

- 珠宝感两件套：`strip softbox drawing an unbroken highlight around the band` +
  `black flag carving the dark edge`——高光条与暗缘一起出立体感。
- 钻石/宝石必须写 `sparking facets / points of fire`（火彩）；不写则宝石像玻璃。
- 微距景深极浅：写 `focus-stacked`（焦点堆栈）保住整体清晰，或明确焦点在哪
  一个细节上。
- 美妆小件走清新线：白亚克力 + 窗光 + 水珠；珠宝走深色线：黑丝绒 + 束光。
