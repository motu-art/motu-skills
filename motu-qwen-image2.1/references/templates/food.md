# 食品 / 美食摄影 — food

**何时用**：菜品图、菜单、餐厅宣传、食谱配图、美食博主内容。核心交付物是
「食欲感」。带文字的菜单海报用 [poster.md](poster.md)。

## 填充清单

| 字段 | 设计要点 |
|---|---|
| 食物 | 什么菜/食品（唯一视觉焦点） |
| 摆盘 | 具体方式：碗盘、堆叠、浇汁瞬间 |
| 道具 | 少量：餐具/桌布/木桌/花草；克制 |
| 环境 | 餐厅/厨房/高级摄影棚（多虚化） |
| 角度 | 45 度 / 俯拍 overhead / 特写 close-up |
| 质感 | 酥脆/湿润/油光/蒸汽/食材纹理——逐项写 |
| 光线 | 柔和自然窗光、暖高光、柔影 |
| 相机 | 美食摄影、微距细节、浅景深 |
| 禁止 | 塑料质感、过度饱和、不真实食材、多余餐具、凌乱背景 |

## Qwen 散文骨架

```text
[Medium statement]. [An appetizing overhead / 45-degree / close-up food photograph of] [dish], styled as the sole visual focus of the frame.
[Plating: vessel, arrangement, garnish, sauce moment].
[Texture sentence: crisp, glistening, steam, visible grain or fiber — the appetizing facts].
A few quiet props support without competing: [prop 1], [prop 2], on [table surface].
The environment suggests [kitchen / restaurant / studio] kept blurred and minimal behind.
Light comes from [a large window camera left/back-left] as [soft natural window light] with warm highlights and soft shadows falling across the table.
Shot [angle] with [macro detail on X] on a shallow depth of field, [focus point].
Food photography with appetizing presentation, natural color, honest materials.
Absolutely no plastic-looking food, no oversaturated colors, no unrealistic ingredients, no extra tableware, no cluttered background, no text, no watermark.
```

## 填写要点

- 食欲感 = 质感事实句：`steam rising from the broth`, `glistening charred edges`,
  `crisp lettuce with visible water droplets`。写状态瞬间（浇汁/掰开/夹起）
  比写静态成品更有食欲。
- 暖色窗光是默认光（`soft natural window light, warm highlights`）；俯拍构图
  把道具摆成环形/对角线，别居中堆满。
- 过度饱和是 AI 美食图最大破绽——负面固定 `no oversaturated colors`。
- 中餐汤面类注意写清碗的形态和筷子角度，模型默认偏西餐摆盘。

## 示例（完整可直接提交）

```text
An appetizing 45-degree food photograph of a steaming bowl of tonkotsu ramen, styled as the sole visual focus of the frame. The rich ivory broth fills a dark stoneware bowl, holding a nest of slightly wavy noodles, two halves of a soft-boiled egg with a jammy glowing yolk, thin slices of chashu pork with caramelized edges, bamboo shoots, green onions and a sheet of nori leaning against the rim. The texture reads delicious: steam rising from the broth, glistening fat droplets on the surface, and the pork slices tender with a faint sheen. A few quiet props support without competing: a pair of wooden chopsticks resting on a small ceramic rest and a folded indigo napkin, on a weathered dark walnut table. The environment suggests a cozy ramen shop kept blurred and minimal behind. Light comes from a large window camera back-left as soft natural window light with warm highlights and soft shadows falling across the table. Shot at 45 degrees with macro detail on the egg yolk and pork on a shallow depth of field, focus on the front rim of the bowl. Food photography with appetizing presentation, natural color, honest materials. Absolutely no plastic-looking food, no oversaturated colors, no unrealistic ingredients, no extra tableware, no cluttered background, no text, no watermark.
```
