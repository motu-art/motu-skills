# 自然风光摄影 — landscape

**何时用**：山川、海岸、森林、星空、湖泊、城市天际线等**摄影真实感**风光——
壁纸、旅行内容、挂画、头图。世界观设定的概念图用 [scene.md](scene.md)（概念
艺术质感），有建筑空间为主体的用 [architecture.md](architecture.md)。

## 填充清单

| 字段 | 设计要点 |
|---|---|
| 地貌 | 什么地点类型（峡湾/梯田/海岸/针叶林/城市天际线） |
| 季节时间 | 什么季节 + 黄金时刻/蓝调/正午/星空 |
| 天气 | 晴/雨/雾/雪/风暴云；空气介质 |
| 前中背景 | 各层具体元素（纵深感来源） |
| 光线 | 太阳位置 + 色温 + 逆光/侧光/顺光；体积光 |
| 相机 | 广角 16–24mm（开阔）/ 长焦 70–200mm（压缩层叠） |
| 景深 | 风光默认全景深 hyperfocal（f/8–f/11） |
| 滤镜感 | ND 长曝丝滑水面 / GND 压天空 / 偏振增饱和 |
| 色彩 | 主色/辅色/强调色 |
| 禁止 | 画面文字、水印、不真实物理（两个太阳） |

## Qwen 散文骨架

```text
[Medium statement]. [A landscape photograph of] [landform] in [season], captured at [time of day] in [weather].
In the foreground [concrete element], in the midground [concrete element], and in the background [concrete element], the layers receding into [atmospheric depth].
The sun sits [position], throwing [light character: warm rim light on the ridges / long shadows across the valley], with [mist / haze / storm light] in the air.
Shot on a [16-24mm wide / 70-200mm telephoto compressing the layers] lens at [f/8-f/11] for front-to-back sharpness, [long exposure smoothing the water / polarized clarity], from [camera position and height].
The palette holds [primary], [secondary] and [accent], the mood [epic / serene / moody].
Professional landscape photography with realistic natural light and atmosphere.
Absolutely no text, no watermark, no impossible physics.
```

## 填写要点

- 风光摄影三件套：时刻（黄金/蓝调最出片）+ 前中背景三层 + 光的方向——
  这三样定了，画面就成立了。
- 长焦压缩（`a 200mm lens compressing the layered ridgelines`）是被低估的效果：
  山峦层叠感、月亮超大感都靠它，比广角更「摄影作品」。
- 长曝特效显式写：`a 30-second long exposure turning the sea into smooth mist`。
- 城市「风光」→ 天际线 + 蓝调时刻 + 灯光初亮（`blue hour, city lights coming
  on`）是稳赢组合。

## 示例（完整可直接提交）

```text
A landscape photograph of layered mountain ridgelines in late autumn, captured at blue hour just after sunset under a clearing sky. In the foreground dark silhouetted pines line a still alpine lake with a perfect mirror reflection, in the midground three receding ridges fade from deep indigo into soft slate, and in the background the furthest range dissolves into a pale band of evening haze, the layers receding into atmospheric depth. The sun has just dropped below the ridge, leaving a faint warm afterglow on the highest peaks against the cooling sky, with thin mist settling over the lake surface. Shot on a 200mm telephoto lens compressing the layered ridgelines at f/8 for front-to-back sharpness, from a low lakeside position with the horizon on the upper third. The palette holds deep indigo, slate gray and a fading amber accent on the peaks, the mood vast, quiet and cold. Professional landscape photography with realistic natural light and atmosphere. Absolutely no text, no watermark, no impossible physics, no people.
```
