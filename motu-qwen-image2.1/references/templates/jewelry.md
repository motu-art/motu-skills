# 珠宝 / 美妆小件 — jewelry

**何时用**：戒指、项链、耳饰、手表、香水瓶、口红、精华瓶等**小微距 + 高级
光泽**特写。与 [product.md](product.md) 的分界：反射控制与材质光泽是命门，
微距景深、束光、吸光都是这个模板的专属工具。

## 填充清单

| 字段 | 设计要点 |
|---|---|
| 品类 | 戒指/项链/香水瓶/口红/精华… |
| 材质 | K金/铂金/银、宝石切工（oval cut/梨形）、磨砂玻璃、漆面 |
| 状态 | 悬浮/卧放/佩戴局部（手模耳模） |
| 背景 | 黑丝绒/白亚克力/水/石材/镜面 |
| 反射控制 | 长条柔光高光沿金属、黑卡吸光勾暗缘 |
| 微距 | 100mm macro、极浅景深、焦点在爪镶/刻字/切面 |
| 细节 | 水珠、气泡、飘丝、指纹禁绝 |
| 光线 | 束光/点光 + 黑背景 = 珠宝感；窗光 + 白背景 = 清新美妆 |
| 禁止 | 错误结构、塑料金属感、灰尘指纹、杂乱反射、文字水印 |

## Qwen 散文骨架

```text
[Medium statement]. [A macro product photograph of] a [category] in [material detail: metal, stone cut, finish].
The piece is presented [floating upright / resting on ... / worn on a hand model's finger], its [focal detail: claw setting / engraved band / facet line] as the point of sharpest focus.
The stage is [background and surface: black velvet / brushed acrylic / wet slate].
Lighting is controlled like jewelry catalog work: [a long strip softbox drawing an unbroken highlight along the metal], [a black flag carving the dark edge for contrast], and [a focused beam sparking the stone's facets].
Fine details sell the picture: [water droplets / drifting silk ribbon / a single bubble], with zero dust and zero fingerprints.
Shot on a 100mm macro lens at f/8 focus-stacked, extreme close-up with the background falling to pure [black / white].
The palette holds [colors], the mood [luxurious / clean / sensual].
Absolutely no incorrect structure, no plasticky metal, no dust, no fingerprints, no distracting reflections, no text, no watermark.
```

## 填写要点

- 珠宝感两句话：`a long strip softbox drawing an unbroken highlight along the
  metal` + `a black flag carving the dark edge`——高光条与暗缘一起出立体感。
- 钻石/宝石必须写 `sparking facets`（火彩）；不写则宝石像玻璃。
- 微距景深极浅：写 `focus-stacked`（焦点堆栈）保住整体清晰，或明确焦点在哪
  一个细节上。
- 美妆小件走清新线：白亚克力 + 窗光 + 水珠；珠宝走深色线：黑丝绒 + 束光。

## 示例（完整可直接提交）

```text
A macro product photograph of a solitaire engagement ring in brushed platinum with an oval-cut diamond. The piece is presented upright on a piece of wet black slate, the claw setting and the diamond's facet line as the point of sharpest focus. The stage is deep black velvet with a faint reflection of the ring on the polished slate. Lighting is controlled like jewelry catalog work: a long strip softbox drawing an unbroken highlight around the platinum band, a black flag carving the dark lower edge for contrast, and a focused beam sparking the stone's facets into points of fire. Fine details sell the picture: two tiny water droplets on the slate beside the ring, with zero dust and zero fingerprints. Shot on a 100mm macro lens at f/8 focus-stacked, extreme close-up with the background falling to pure black. The palette holds cool platinum white and deep black, the mood luxurious and precise. Absolutely no incorrect structure, no plasticky metal, no dust, no fingerprints, no distracting reflections, no text, no watermark.
```
