# 动物 / 宠物摄影 — animal

**何时用**：宠物摄影（猫/狗/异宠）、野生动物感画面、萌宠商业图、宠物品牌
内容。命门是**眼睛清晰 + 解剖正确**。

## 填充清单

| 字段 | 设计要点 |
|---|---|
| 物种品种 | 什么动物、什么品种（英短/柯基/橘猫） |
| 特征 | 毛色花纹、年龄感、体型 |
| 动作 | 在做什么（扑/卧/回眸/奔跑）——决定神态 |
| 神态 | 耳朵/胡须/瞳孔状态；**眼睛必须清晰锐利** |
| 环境 | 室内窗光/草地/影棚纯色/自然栖息地 |
| 光线 | 窗光/逆光轮廓毛边/柔光棚拍 |
| 相机 | 70–200mm、浅景深、平视机位（宠物视角） |
| 毛发质感 | 逆光下的绒毛边、可见毛流 |
| 禁止 | 错误解剖、多余/畸形肢体、畸形五官、塑料毛发感 |

## Qwen 散文骨架

```text
[Medium statement]. [A pet / wildlife photograph of] a [breed] [animal], [coat pattern and build].
[Action with observable detail], with [ear/whisker/pupil state] and [gaze direction], both eyes tack-sharp.
The setting is [environment kept simple / natural habitat detail].
Light comes from [window light / backlight from ... / soft studio light], rimming the fur with [glow along the edges] and modeling the coat texture.
Shot on a [70-200mm] lens at [f/2.2] from [eye level with the animal], a [medium close-up / full body] with shallow depth of field melting the background.
[Palette and mood].
Absolutely no wrong anatomy, no extra limbs, no deformed face, no plastic-looking fur, no text, no watermark.
```

## 填写要点

- `both eyes tack-sharp` 必写——动物摄影的第一评价标准就是眼睛；失焦眼=废片。
- 平视机位（`at the animal's eye level`）是宠物片显专业的最大单一变量，默认
  俯拍会显得像手机随手拍。
- 逆光 + 绒毛边（`backlight rimming the fur with a soft glow`）出氛围；正面
  柔光出清洁商业感。
- 动物解剖负面四连固定：`no wrong anatomy, no extra limbs, no deformed face` +
  品种混写要小心（写英短就别加折耳特征）。

## 示例（完整可直接提交）

```text
A pet photograph of a British Shorthair cat with a dense blue-gray coat, round copper eyes and plush cheeks. The cat lies with front paws tucked under its chest in a classic loaf position on a pale oak floor, one ear rotated toward the camera and whiskers fanned forward, gaze level and direct, both eyes tack-sharp. The setting is a bright minimalist living room with a linen sofa blurred far behind. Light comes from a large window camera left, backlighting a fine halo along the rounded ear edges and modeling the dense coat with soft top-light and gentle shadow under the chin. Shot on a 135mm lens at f/2.2 at the cat's eye level, a medium close-up with shallow depth of field melting the floor and sofa into warm neutral tones. The palette holds blue-gray, pale oak and cream, the mood calm and endearing. Absolutely no wrong anatomy, no extra limbs, no deformed face, no plastic-looking fur, no text, no watermark.
```
