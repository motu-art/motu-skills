# 场景 / 环境概念图 — scene

**何时用**：游戏场景、电影场景、世界观设定、科幻/奇幻、城市、自然景观、氛围
空镜。无人或人只作为比例参照。室内空间设计用 [architecture.md](architecture.md)，
有人物主体用 [people-scene.md](people-scene.md)。

## 填充清单

| 字段 | 设计要点 |
|---|---|
| 场景类型 | 一个什么场景 |
| 时间 | 清晨/正午/黄昏/夜晚 |
| 地点 | 具体地点 |
| 环境三层 | 前景 / 中景 / 背景各是什么元素 |
| 空间 | 规模（狭小/开阔/宏大）；纵深明显、层次明确 |
| 建筑/结构 | 风格 + 主要结构 2–3 个 |
| 天气 | 晴/雨/雾/雪；空气中的介质（雾气/尘埃/雨滴） |
| 光线 | 主光源 + 方向 + 阴影性质（长/柔/体积光） |
| 色彩 | 主色/辅色/强调色 |
| 氛围 | 宁静/神秘/宏大/温暖/危险/未来感 |
| 相机 | 广角电影构图、24mm、深透视 |
| 画质 | high-detail environment concept art、atmospheric perspective |

## Qwen 散文骨架

```text
[Medium statement]. An environment concept art of [scene] at [time] in [specific place].
In the foreground [concrete element], in the midground [concrete element], and in the background [concrete element], creating strong visual depth with distinct layers and [spatial scale: vast / intimate / monumental].
The architecture follows [style], with [structure 1], [structure 2], and [structure 3].
The weather is [condition], with [mist / drifting dust / rain streaks] hanging in the air.
Light comes from [source] at [direction], producing [long soft shadows / volumetric light shafts] through the [air medium].
Shot as a wide establishing composition on a 24mm wide-angle lens from a low angle, deep perspective with atmospheric haze softening the distance.
The palette holds [primary], [secondary] and [accent], the mood [serene / mysterious / grand / dangerous].
High-detail environment concept art with realistic materials, rich environmental storytelling and atmospheric perspective.
Absolutely no prominent figures, no text, no watermark.
```

## 填写要点

- 前中背景三层**各写具体元素**，这是纵深感的来源；只写「很有层次」没用。
- 大气透视 `atmospheric perspective softening the distance` + 雾气是概念图质感
  的快捷键。
- 24mm 广角 + 低机位 = 宏大；高空俯瞰写 `aerial view, high angle`。
- 氛围决定用光方向：神秘 = 逆光+雾；宏大 = 低角度侧逆光+长影；宁静 = 漫射柔光。

## 示例（完整可直接提交）

```text
An environment concept art of an ancient cliffside monastery in a remote mountain valley at dawn. In the foreground weathered stone steps wind upward between pine branches draped with hanging moss, in the midground a terraced monastery complex of whitewashed walls, dark timber balconies and fluttering prayer flags clings to the sheer granite cliff, and in the background layered peaks fade into pale mist, creating strong visual depth with distinct layers and a vast vertical scale. The architecture follows Himalayan stone-and-timber style, with a golden rooftop finial, timber walkways cantilevered over the void, and small square windows cut into whitewashed walls. The weather is clear and cold, with thin mist drifting through the valley and slow-moving cloud layers below the peaks. Light comes from the low sun behind the right ridge, producing volumetric light shafts through the mist and long soft shadows down the stone steps. Shot as a wide establishing composition on a 24mm wide-angle lens from a low angle at the base of the steps, deep perspective with atmospheric haze softening the distance. The palette holds pale gold, cool slate gray and a muted jade accent, the mood sacred, hushed and timeless. High-detail environment concept art with realistic materials, rich environmental storytelling and atmospheric perspective. Absolutely no prominent figures, no text, no watermark.
```
